Beekeeping · Builder

BroodLink

Apiary software for beekeeping nerds. You log a hive inspection by voice, and BroodLink suggests the next procedure step by step, with every recommendation tied back to university extension research. Optional Raspberry Pi and Pico sensor nodes track temperature, humidity and hive weight. It's free for hobbyist beekeepers.
